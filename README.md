# HR Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition patterns,attrition rate, department/job role breakdowns, income comparison, and age trends, with data cleaning in Power Query and a custom DAX measure.


![Dashboard Screenshot](HR-Attrition-Dashboard-Overview.png)

## Key Insights

- Overall attrition rate is 16.1% (237 of 1,470 employees)
- Research & Development has the highest attrition by department, and also the lowest average income among departments — suggesting a possible link worth investigating
- Laboratory Technician, Sales Executive, and Research Scientist are the job roles with the highest attrition
- Job satisfaction shows no clean linear relationship with attrition
- Attrition peaks sharply around ages 29–31, concentrated mostly under age 35

## Tools
Power BI, DAX, Power Query
